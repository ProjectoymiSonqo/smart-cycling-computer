# Design Decisions

## Decision 001
### Why nRF52840?

Status: Accepted

Reason
- Native BLE support
- ANT+ ecosystem
- Fast prototyping

---

## Decision 002
### Why Pitot Tube?

Status: Accepted

Reason
- GPS cannot measure headwind.
- Relative airspeed is required for CdA estimation.

---

## Decision 003
### Why Modular Front Light?

Status: Accepted

Reason
- Keep the cycling computer replaceable.
- Accessories evolve independently.
