# ANT+

## Purpose

Provides communication with cycling accessories.

---

## Why ANT+?

Selected because:

- Industry standard in cycling
- Low power consumption
- Native support on nRF52840

---

## Supported Devices

- Heart Rate Monitor
- Power Meter
- Speed Sensor (Future)
- Cadence Sensor (Future)

---

## Responsibilities

- Sensor pairing
- Sensor management
- Data acquisition
