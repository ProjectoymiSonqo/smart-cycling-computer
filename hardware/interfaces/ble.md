# Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)

## Purpose

Provides wireless communication between the cycling computer and the mobile application.

---

## Why BLE?

Selected because:

- Low power consumption
- Native support on smartphones
- Supported by nRF52840

---

## Responsibilities

- Device configuration
- Ride synchronization
- Firmware update (Future)
- Notifications
