# MAX17048 Fuel Gauge

## Purpose

Monitors battery state of charge.

---

## Why Selected?

Selected because:

- No battery learning required
- Low power consumption
- Simple I2C interface
- Suitable for portable devices

---

## Interfaces

I2C

Output

- Battery Voltage
- State of Charge (SOC)

---

## Responsibilities

- Battery monitoring
- Remaining capacity estimation
- Low battery warning
