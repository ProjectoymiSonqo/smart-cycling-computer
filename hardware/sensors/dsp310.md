# DPS310

## Purpose

Provides atmospheric pressure for altitude estimation.

---

## Why Selected?

Selected because:

- High precision pressure sensor
- Small package
- Low power
- Simple I2C interface

---

## Interfaces

I2C

Output

- Pressure
- Temperature

---

## Responsibilities

- Altitude estimation
- Climbing information
- Sensor fusion with GNSS
