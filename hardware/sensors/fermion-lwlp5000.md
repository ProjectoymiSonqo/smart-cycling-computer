# Fermion LWLP5000 Differential Pressure Sensor

## Purpose

Measures pressure difference generated by the Pitot tube for relative airspeed estimation.

---

## Why Selected?

Selected because:

- Small size
- High sensitivity
- Digital output
- Suitable pressure range for cycling applications

---

## Interfaces

I2C

---

## Responsibilities

- Differential pressure measurement
- Relative airspeed estimation
- CdA calculation input
